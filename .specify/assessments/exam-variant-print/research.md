## Data & Constraints

- QR currently `{v:1,sid,code}`; independent sheet has sid null — source: lib/grading/sheet-template.ts, cited/high.
- Wrong-session OMR is currently only a warning, and answers can still persist against active rubric — source: app/api/grading/submissions/[id]/omr/route.ts; independent read-only research agent confirmed lines74–122, cited/high. Must fail closed for new published sheets.
- Rubric supports A–D single choice and essay, not structured true/false/short answer — source: lib/grading/rubric.ts, cited/high.
- Question bank preserves source ranges but does not establish semantic independence; metadata answers may be null. Its AI extraction cannot prove safe arbitrary shuffle — source: lib/question-bank source/candidate validation, read-only research, cited/high.
- Current one-page standalone sheet limited to60 A–D. This is a deliberate application limit, not a statement about Vietnamese exam regulations — source: app/api/editor/export-sheet/route.ts, cited/high.
- Blind number substitution can change domains, diagrams and distractor uniqueness. Need bounded parametric recipes and recomputation rather than arbitrary HTML search/replace — reasoning/ASSUMPTION high confidence.

## Evidence Against the Idea

- Arbitrary HTML, dependent passages and cross-references cannot safely be shuffled with regex alone. Unsupported layouts must be refused or stay unshuffled — cited code constraints above/high.
- Numeric variants cannot be certified equivalent just because answers were generated by AI; unlimited automatic transformations would be misleading — reasoning/high, not empirical market evidence.
- QR unreadability and incorrect variant acceptance can yield wrong grades, making conservative rejection more important than frictionless guessing — cited OMR route/high.

## Gaps & Open Questions

- Exact teacher printing volumes and preferred code schemes unknown; choose editable defaults and bounded batch size, label assumptions.
- Need deterministic supported structural extraction with explicit grouped/pinned handling and teacher review.
- Need actual browser/API tests for publish rollback, owner isolation, stale versions and QR mismatch.
